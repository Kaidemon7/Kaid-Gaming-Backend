Basketball Stars

Our most-played game right now, Basketball Stars is a fast, flashy 1-on-1 showdown you can play solo or with a friend on the same keyboard. Pick a club, drop into a tournament, and chain dribbles, steals, and rim-rattling dunks into the kind of highlight plays that keep a match alive to the final buzzer.

The skill comes from timing: hold to charge a shot, release at the top of the arc, and read your opponent before they read you. In 1 Player mode you climb tournaments and dial the difficulty up as you improve; in 2 Player mode you settle who's really the best at your table. Easy to start, deep enough to argue about — that's why it sits at the top of our charts.

Tap Tap Shots

If you like short sessions with a high skill ceiling, Tap Tap Shots is almost impossible to put down. Flick the ball into the hoop with a single tap, then keep the rhythm going as the basket slides to a new spot after every make.

Miss once and your streak resets, so every shot carries weight — one clean run can rocket you up the high-score board, and one greedy tap can end it. It's the perfect “just one more try” game for a quick break between classes.

Love Tester

More of a playful fortune-telling machine than a game, Love Tester puts a modern spin on the classic “he loves me, he loves me not” ritual. Type in two names, hit the button, and watch the meter deliver its verdict.

It's light, silly, and made for sharing — the kind of thing that gets passed around a group of friends until everyone's been “tested.” No skill required, just a few seconds of fun and a result worth laughing about.

Snow Rider 3D

One of our most-loved arcade runners, Snow Rider 3D drops you onto a sled and sends you flying down an endless, snow-covered mountain. Weave between snowdrifts, ducked-under logs, and snapping gates, and the further you survive, the faster it all comes at you. Scoop up the gift boxes scattered across the slope to unlock new sleds — from candy canes to full-on rockets — and turn each run into a fresh shot at your best distance.

Moto X3M

Moto X3M is a time-trial bike racer built around one addictive loop: memorize the course, then shave seconds off it. Each stage is a little obstacle gauntlet of ramps, loops, swinging wrecking balls, and explosive barrels, and your clock only stops when you cross the line.

The trick is in the air — tilt back to flip and you land faster and earn time, but over-rotate and you'll eat the dirt. Chasing that three-star, no-crash run across all the levels is what keeps players coming back for “one more attempt.”

Traffic Jam 3D & Highway Traffic

Driving fans have two very different favorites here. Traffic Jam 3D is all about flow — threading your car through dense city traffic at speed without clipping a bumper. Highway Traffic turns up the tension, daring you to weave between cars that brake, drift, and accelerate without warning. Both reward patience and nerve, and both keep score by distance, so there's always a personal best to chase. Want to slide sideways instead? Drift Hunters 2024 is the drift specialist of the bunch.

Highway Traffic

If endless, white-knuckle driving is your thing, Highway Traffic delivers it in pure form. You ride the line between lanes of fast-moving cars, and the closer you shave past them, the more your score climbs.

There's no finish line — only your own reflexes deciding how far you get. Switch lanes late, read the gaps, and resist the urge to floor it, and you'll quietly rack up one of the most satisfying high scores on the site.

Run 3 & Skill Favorites

For pure reflex tests, start with Run 3 — a gravity-bending sprint through a crumbling space tunnel where the walls become the floor and one wrong step drops you into the void. It's equal parts platformer and puzzle as you learn each tunnel's rhythm.

From there, Geometry Vibes 3D and Backflip Dive 3D reward precise timing and steady nerve, while Extreme Run 3D keeps the obstacle courses coming. Want to switch off and unwind instead? Watermelon Suika is a cozy merge puzzle that's deceptively hard to stop playing.
