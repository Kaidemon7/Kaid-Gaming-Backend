Stickman Ragdoll

If you want to play with physics, you could have some crazy fun with Stickman Ragdoll. Draw the Bridge

When you want some arcade fun, Draw the Bridge has the simple game mechanic of drag and draw to get the vehicle from A to B. Slither Snakes

Do you love bright, neon graphics combined with simple, intuitive gameplay? Well, the good news is that Slither Snakes will tick all of the right boxes. You can’t talk about 2D games without mentioning a cool take on a classic like Nova Idle Clicker, which is based on old school pong.
