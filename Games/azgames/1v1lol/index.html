What is 1v1.LOL (1v1 LOL)?

1v1.LOL (also commonly known as 1v1 LOL or 1v1lol) is a fast-paced, competitive third-person builder-shooter game that lets you duel online players worldwide directly in your web browser. Created by JustPlay.LOL and kept alive since the November 2025 shutdown by the community-run Reloaded servers, the game distills the core mechanics of battle royale games down to what matters most: rapid building, high-ground control, and crisp shotgun aim.

With zero download or installation required, 1v1.LOL loads in seconds and delivers ultra-smooth 60+ FPS gameplay on desktops, laptops, and Chromebooks.

Game Modes in 1v1 LOL

The lobby asks two separate questions. Solo or Duo — the bar left of the PLAY button — decides who is on your side. Change Mode decides what you are playing:

  • 1v1 Clash: The purest test of close-quarters combat. Two duelists, a tight arena and trophies on the line — take walls, edit peek angles, land clean headshots.
  • Battle Royale: Drop into a sprawling open map, scavenge weapons and ammunition from chests, farm materials, and survive as the last player standing.
  • Free Build: An empty map with unlimited materials and nobody shooting at you. Where 90s, side-jumps and ramp rushes get drilled before they get used.
  • BR Practice, Zombies & Aim Trainer: The Practice tab — a solo run of the battle-royale map, a wave-survival mode, and a straight aim range.
  • Custom Games: A private room you control, joined by code. Set the mode, invite exactly who you want.
  • Solo or Duo: The team-size bar left of PLAY. Duo puts a partner beside you in whichever mode you queued. For a proper two-against-two with its own ladder, Box Fight and Zone Wars, take the 2v2 game instead — the switch above the player.

How to Play 1v1.LOL Online (No Download)

  1. Click the Play button above to load the WebGL client in your browser.
  2. Select your favorite game mode (Box Fight is recommended for quick warmups).
  3. Use your pickaxe (G key) to harvest wood, brick, and metal from environmental objects.
  4. Build ramps (C) and walls (Q) to secure high-ground advantage over your opponent.
  5. Switch swiftly to your shotgun or rifle to deal damage before taking cover.

Pro Tips to Win More 1v1.LOL Duels

  • Always Fight for High Ground: High ground provides cleaner headshot angles and natural cover behind your own ramps. If your opponent builds above you, ramp outwards diagonally to regain the advantage.
  • Wall Before Ramp: Placing a protective wall before dropping a ramp prevents opponents from shooting your ramp out from underneath you.
  • Master Right-Hand Peeks: In third-person shooters, your camera sits over the right shoulder. Peeking around right corners exposes less of your hitbox than peeking left.
  • Maintain 500+ Materials: Running out of wood mid-duel leaves you defenseless. Always farm between skirmishes.

Is 1v1.LOL Shut Down? What You Are Playing Here

Yes — the official game is. JustPlay.LOL switched the 1v1.LOL servers off on 3 November 2025, taking the Steam build, the mobile apps and the original site with it. The game on this page is 1v1.LOL Reloaded, a community-run revival that rebuilt the server side so the same client can play again: same building, weapons and maps, with matchmaking, parties and ranked progression running on servers the players maintain. It is not affiliated with the original studio, and nothing from an old official account carries over — you start fresh, as a guest or with a Google sign-in that saves rank and unlocks.

What that means day to day: queues are shorter at US and EU evening hours and slower late at night, the shop catalogue is new, and the standalone 2v2 ladder is gone (the 2v2 game above the player covers that). The full story — the announcement, why it closed, and how Reloaded came about — is in Is 1v1.LOL shut down?

Frequently Asked Questions About 1v1.LOL

Is 1v1.LOL free to play online here?

Yes, 1v1.LOL is 100% free to play directly in your web browser. There are no downloads, installs, or mandatory account registrations required. Simply press Play to jump straight into a match.

What is 1v1.LOL (1v1 LOL)?

1v1.LOL is a competitive third-person builder-shooter game developed by JustPlay.LOL. It combines fast-paced tactical shooting with real-time building mechanics (ramps, walls, floors, and roofs), allowing players to outbuild and outgun opponents in intense 1v1 duels.

How do I play 1v1.LOL unblocked at school or on Chromebooks?

Because this site runs the pure WebGL browser build over standard HTTPS, 1v1.LOL works seamlessly on school Chromebooks, laptops, and restricted networks without needing software installations or admin privileges.

What are the default controls and keybinds in 1v1.LOL?

Move with WASD, aim and shoot with the Mouse, jump with Spacebar. Build keybinds: Wall (Q), Floor (F), Ramp (C), Roof (V), and Edit (E). Switch weapons with keys 1–5, and pull out your pickaxe with G to farm building materials.

Which game mode is best for beginners in 1v1 LOL?

Start in Free Build under the Practice tab: an empty map with unlimited materials and nobody shooting at you, which is where ramps, walls and edits become muscle memory. Aim Trainer is the other half of the warm-up. Once both feel automatic, 1v1 Clash is the mode to take them into.

Can I play 1v1.LOL with friends in 2v2 or private matches?

Yes. Set the lobby bar from Solo to Duo and you queue as a pair; to choose the partner rather than take a random one, invite them from the friends icon or open Change Mode then Custom and share the room code. Reloaded no longer has a standalone 2v2 mode, so for a real two-against-two ladder use the 2v2 game on our 2v2 page — the switch sits directly above the player.

Is 1v1.LOL shut down?

The official servers are. JustPlay.LOL closed 1v1.LOL on 3 November 2025 and the Steam, mobile and original web versions stopped working that day. The game on this page is 1v1.LOL Reloaded, a community-run revival that rebuilt the servers so the same client plays again. Old official accounts and purchases did not carry over.

What is 1v1.LOL Reloaded and is it the same game?

Reloaded is the same Unity client — identical building, weapons, maps and controls — connected to servers run by players rather than the original studio. It is not affiliated with JustPlay.LOL. Ranked 1v1 Clash, Battle Royale, Zone Wars, Build Race and custom party modes are live; you can play as a guest or sign in with Google to keep rank and unlocks.

Is 1v1.LOL the same as Fortnite?

1v1.LOL was inspired by the core building and shooting mechanics of Fortnite, but strips away long loot phases, vehicle clutter, and massive download sizes, focusing purely on rapid 1v1 combat and aim-building mechanics.

How can I increase FPS and reduce lag in 1v1.LOL?

Ensure Hardware Acceleration is enabled in your browser settings (Chrome/Edge/Brave/Firefox). Close background tabs and applications, and play in Fullscreen mode for maximum GPU performance.
